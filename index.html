Euichan Heo

M.S. Candidate, CAMEL, Pusan National University · Busan, Republic of Korea

I’m a robotics & AI researcher at CAMEL (Computer And Machine Engaged Laboratory) in Busan, Republic of Korea. At CAMEL I’ve worked on bimanual manipulation, imitation learning, and constrained policy learning — from visuomotor policies and VLA models to QP-based control. See MY JOURNEY for the full path, or PROJECT for what I’ve built.
