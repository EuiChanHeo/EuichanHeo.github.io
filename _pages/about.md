Robotics researcher interested in **imitation learning**, **optimization**, and intelligent robotic manipulation. I'm currently an M.S. candidate at [CAMEL Lab](https://pnu-camel.oopy.io/) (Computer And Machine Engaged Lab), Pusan National University, working on imitation-learning-based control for bimanual and humanoid robots.

I care about robots that can learn manipulation skills from demonstration and act reliably in the physical world — from a strain-gauge guide-dog harness to visuomotor policies for humanoid manufacturing. See [My Journey](/cv/) for the full path, or [Projects](/projects/) for what I've built.
